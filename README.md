# CCTest - The Universe

The universe is the totality of all space, time, matter, and energy that exists. It began approximately 13.8 billion years ago with the Big Bang — an incredible expansion from an extremely hot, dense state.

## Key Facts

- **Age:** ~13.8 billion years
- **Observable diameter:** ~93 billion light-years
- **Composition:** ~68% dark energy, ~27% dark matter, ~5% ordinary matter
- **Estimated galaxies:** Over 2 trillion

## Structure

The universe is organized in a vast cosmic web of galaxy clusters, filaments, and voids. From the smallest subatomic particles to the largest superclusters, every scale reveals extraordinary complexity and beauty.

The more we explore, the more we realize how much remains unknown.

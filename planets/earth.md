# Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known planet to harbor life. It formed approximately 4.5 billion years ago and is the densest planet in the Solar System.

## Key Facts

- **Distance from Sun:** ~149.6 million km (1 AU)
- **Diameter:** ~12,742 km
- **Mass:** 5.97 × 10²⁴ kg
- **Orbital period:** 365.25 days
- **Rotation period:** 23 hours, 56 minutes
- **Moons:** 1 (the Moon)
- **Surface temperature:** -88°C to 58°C

## What Makes Earth Special

- **Liquid water:** Oceans cover about 71% of the surface
- **Atmosphere:** Nitrogen-oxygen mix that supports life and shields from radiation
- **Magnetic field:** Protects the planet from solar wind
- **Plate tectonics:** Continuously reshapes the surface and recycles carbon

## Fun Fact

Earth is the only planet not named after a Greek or Roman deity. Its name comes from Old English and Germanic words meaning "the ground."
